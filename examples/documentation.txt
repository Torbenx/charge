-- Variable kinds: let, mut, inout, out, destroy.
   Where the 'let' and 'mut' apply to all variables and the others only to parameters.
-- 'let' is the default and can be omitted.
-- Identity property: identity => substance => value

-- Properties of identifier expression naming variable:

           | let            | inout/out         | mut/destroy
   --------|----------------|-------------------|------------------
    struct | value          | substance/mutable | identity/mutable
    object | identity/const | identity /mutable | identity/muatble

-- Initialized at begin/end:

           | let        | inout   | out    | mut     | destroy
   --------|------------|---------|--------|---------|---------
    struct | yes*/undef | yes/yes | no/yes | yes*/no | yes/no
    object | non-owning | yes/yes | no/yes | yes*/no | yes/no

   *The initialization of block 'let' and 'mut' variables can be deferred.
